What we automate

Every business is different, but the same kinds of work show up everywhere. These are the areas where AI usually saves the most time.

  • Client communication

    Every inquiry by email, WhatsApp or your website gets a fast reply. Common questions are answered automatically, and everything else goes to the right person.

  • Follow-ups and sales

    Proposals and quotes followed up on time, so good opportunities don't go quiet.

  • Scheduling

    Clients book themselves in, get reminders and reschedule without the back-and-forth.

  • Billing and payments

    Invoices created in the invoicing software you already use, and payment reminders sent on schedule.

  • Documents and data entry

    Forms, contracts and notes are read and filed in the right place, with anything unclear flagged for a person to check.

  • Reports and insights

    The numbers you check every week, pulled from your tools and summarized for you.

  • Marketing content

    Posts, newsletters and reviews drafted in your voice, ready for your approval.

  • An AI assistant for your team

    A private assistant that knows your procedures and documents and answers your team's questions.

AI you can trust

Plenty of business owners are wary of AI, and that's reasonable. Here's how we make sure you can trust everything we build.

  • A person stays in charge

    Automations follow rules you approve. Anything sensitive or unusual comes to you or your team first.

  • Business-grade tools only

    We build on established providers, on business plans whose terms say your data isn't used to train their AI models.

  • Your data stays yours

    Access is limited to what's needed, and every client gets a confidentiality and data-processing agreement in line with Israeli privacy law.

  • You own everything

    Accounts are set up in your business's name. You can see, pause or switch off any automation at any time.

  • AI that says it's AI

    Any assistant that talks to your customers introduces itself as one, and hands over to a person on request.

  • Start small, prove it

    We begin with one workflow, measure the result, and only expand when it's clearly working.
