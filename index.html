AI you can trust

Plenty of business owners are wary of AI, and that's reasonable. Here's how we make sure you can trust everything we build.

  • A person stays in charge

    Automations follow rules you approve. Anything sensitive or unusual comes to you or your team first.

  • Business-grade tools only

    We build on established providers, on business plans whose terms say your data isn't used to train their AI models.

  • Your data stays yours

    Access is limited to what's needed, and every client gets a confidentiality and data-processing agreement in line with Israeli privacy law.

  • You own everything

    Accounts are set up in your business's name. You can see, pause or switch off any automation at any time.

  • AI that says it's AI

    Any assistant that talks to your customers introduces itself as one, and hands over to a person on request.

  • Start small, prove it

    We begin with one workflow, measure the result, and only expand when it's clearly working.
